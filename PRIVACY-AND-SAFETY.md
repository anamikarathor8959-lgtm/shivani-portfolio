# Privacy and responsible-use notes

- Do not enter student names, phone numbers, email addresses, home addresses, school IDs, passwords, precise location, photographs or sensitive personal information.
- The prototype does not create student accounts or store student profiles.
- Local activity counters are stored in the browser on the current device. They are not academic grades or validated learning measures.
- Requests sent to the configured AI provider are processed under that provider's API terms and data controls. Review current provider policies before a real classroom pilot.
- The server sets `store: false` on Responses API calls; this is not a substitute for reviewing provider retention, abuse-monitoring and account data-control policies.
- AI-generated content can be wrong or unsuitable. A qualified teacher must review material before classroom use.
- Do not use the tool as the sole source for medical, legal, safety, counselling or other high-stakes advice.
- Values-based content should remain inclusive, respectful and non-coercive.
- Before any pilot with minors, obtain appropriate school/guardian approvals and follow applicable child-safety, privacy and education requirements.
