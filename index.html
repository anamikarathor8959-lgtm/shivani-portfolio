Modern Minds, Rooted Souls

ARISE connects academic learning with thoughtful action. Use these inclusive reflection prompts after a lesson:

  • Curiosity: What new question did this topic make you think about?
  • Empathy: How could this learning help us understand another person's experience?
  • Responsibility: What is one practical, safe action connected to this topic?
  • Integrity: How can we check whether information is reliable?
  • Reflection: What would you like to learn more about, and why?

These prompts are inclusive and do not require students to follow any particular religious or spiritual belief.
