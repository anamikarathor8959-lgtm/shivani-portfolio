ARISE — Modern Minds, Rooted Souls

ARISE is an education concept that brings entrepreneurship and spirituality into conversation. The idea is to help students develop modern skills, creative confidence and an entrepreneurial mindset while staying connected to values, purpose and their roots.

North star: Prepare young people not only to find opportunities, but also to create them responsibly.
