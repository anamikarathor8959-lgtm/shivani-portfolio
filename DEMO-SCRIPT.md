# ARISE — 3-minute demonstration script

**0:00–0:25 | Introduction**  
“ARISE stands for *Modern Minds, Rooted Souls*. It is an AI-supported teacher assistant designed to help teachers prepare learning resources and help students understand concepts in simple, level-appropriate language. It also brings values such as curiosity, empathy and responsibility into the learning experience.”

**0:25–1:15 | Teacher Studio**  
“Here, a teacher selects a subject, class, topic, learning objective and language. ARISE can draft an explanation, real-life examples, a classroom activity, a quiz with an answer key, a lesson plan, a worksheet or a values reflection. I’ll use Water Conservation for Class 8 and generate a simple explanation.”

**1:15–2:00 | AI Doubt Solver**  
“Students can ask a question and receive a step-by-step explanation. For example: ‘Why is groundwater conservation important?’ The response is adapted to the selected class level. This is intended to support learning, not replace a teacher.”

**2:00–2:30 | Learning insights and values**  
“The prototype shows basic activity counts locally in the browser. These are not grades or proof of learning. The Values & Reflection section offers prompts around empathy, integrity and responsibility, without imposing a particular religious belief.”

**2:30–3:00 | Responsible AI and close**  
“ARISE uses a secure backend so the AI key is not exposed in the browser. Teachers must review generated content, and users should never enter student personal information. The next step is a supervised pilot with educators, curriculum review and usability feedback. Our aim is to make technology support both effective learning and thoughtful human values.”

## Suggested demo prompts
- Subject: Science | Class: 8 | Topic: Water Conservation | Resource: Simple explanation
- Subject: Science | Class: 8 | Topic: Water Conservation | Resource: Quiz & answer key
- Doubt Solver question: “Why should we protect groundwater?”
